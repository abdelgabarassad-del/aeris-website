Sponsors & Partners

Innovating flight systems and aerospace robotics is made possible through the support of our network. We proudly collaborate with academic leaders, cutting-edge industries, local stores, and individual flight system patrons.

Strategic & Academic Partners

AASTMT - El Alamein Branch

Principal Academic Partner

Providing state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, academic mentorship, and critical structural resources for engineering, prototyping, and aerospace flight system research at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT) El Alamein campus.

College of Engineering & Technology

Faculty Partner

The College of Engineering & Technology (CoET) at AASTMT serves as the academic home of A.E.R.I.S., providing our team with world-class engineering curricula, faculty guidance, and access to advanced research infrastructure that fuels our UAV innovations.

Research Partners

Beyond Space

Aerospace Research Collaborator

A.E.R.I.S. is proudly partnered with Beyond Space on a joint research paper exploring the design and feasibility of a UAV specifically engineered for Mars exploration. Together, we are pushing the frontiers of extraterrestrial aerial vehicle technology.

Technology & Local Partners

Robotronics

Online Electronics Partner

An exceptional online electronics retailer offering a vast selection of robotic kits, avionics sensors, and flight controllers. Robotronics generously supports A.E.R.I.S. by providing exclusive member discounts on their entire product catalog.

Electra Store

Local & Online Electronics Partner

A premier electronics supplier featuring both an extensive online shop and a physical retail store. Electra Store fuels our UAV designs with high-grade components and power distribution hardware at tailored discount rates.
