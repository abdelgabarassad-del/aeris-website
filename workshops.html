Our Workshops

UAV Design & Aerodynamics

Explore the fundamentals of flight dynamics, airfoil selection, and aircraft configuration. This workshop provides students with hands-on experience in calculating lift, drag, and thrust requirements, allowing participants to conceptualize and optimize unmanned aerial vehicles for various flight missions.

Hands-on Engineering

Avionics & Autonomous Systems

Delve into the brain of unmanned systems. Participants learn how to configure open-source flight controllers like Pixhawk and ArduPilot, calibrate critical sensors (GPS, IMUs, compasses), and design autonomous mission pathways for surveying and competitive flight parameters.

Flight Integration

Embedded Systems & Robotics

Understand the hardware-software interface. This training covers microcontroller programming (Arduino, ESP32), sensor telemetry integration, actuator signal processing, and communication protocols (PWM, I2C, SPI) necessary for robust robot operations and custom telemetry designs.

Microcontroller Programming

CAD Modeling & Rapid Prototyping

Transform concepts into physical airframes. Learn advanced 3D modeling using SolidWorks, perform finite element analysis (FEA) to verify structural integrity under load, and explore rapid prototyping techniques such as 3D printing and laser cutting using lightweight composite materials.
