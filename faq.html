Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about A.E.R.I.S., our team structure, technologies, and external activities.

We are an advanced engineering team dedicated to the design, structural optimization, and fabrication of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Our primary focus is on developing highly efficient flight systems, ranging from multirotor drones—such as custom octocopters and quadcopters—to exploratory concepts in high-altitude and orbital mechanics.
To handle complex aerospace projects, A.E.R.I.S. is divided into specialized, cross-functional sub-teams. This includes dedicated mechanical and electrical engineering groups that handle design and hardware, supported by a non-technical division that manages our operations, logistics, and competition planning.
Our workflow relies on industry-standard aerospace tools. We use SolidWorks for CAD modeling, ANSYS Fluent for Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), and MATLAB/Simulink for control systems and orbital simulations. For fabrication, we actively utilize additive manufacturing (3D printing with specialized filaments) and laser cutting for rapid prototyping of airframes and custom components.
