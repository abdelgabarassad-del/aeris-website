Join A.E.R.I.S.

Become part of our specialized aerospace sub-teams. Build custom quadcopters, model aerodynamics, code control algorithms, and shape the future of flight systems.

Recruitment Process Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Once submitted, our division heads will evaluate your profile. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a technical and behavioral interview.

Mechanical Division

Focuses on airframe design, structural optimization, rapid prototyping (3D printing, laser cutting), and balsa wood fabrication. Employs SolidWorks for CAD modeling and ANSYS for CFD analysis.

Electrical Division

Handles embedded hardware integration, custom ESC optimization, telemetry configurations, sensor interfacing, power distribution wiring, and signal diagnostics for absolute flight stability.

Software Division

Works on flight control coding, path planning, autonomous navigation algorithms, web integration, dashboard displays, telemetry databases, and real-time sensor processing models.

TDR & Marketing Division

Responsible for drafting critical technical documentation reports, leading corporate sponsorship outreach, and building the team's public image through strategic media and brand management.

Membership Terms & Conditions

By joining and participating in the Advanced Engineering and Robotics in Flight Systems (A.E.R.I.S.) team, all members are bound by our institutional policies and code of conduct.

1. General Conduct and Expectations

Professionalism: Members must maintain a respectful, professional, and collaborative environment at all times, both in internal meetings and when representing the team externally at competitions, exhibitions, or conferences.

Commitment: Active participation in assigned subteam tasks, design reviews, and project milestones is expected. Consistent communication with team leadership regarding progress and roadblocks is required.

2. Financial Contributions and Non-Refundability

Dues and Costs: Members may be required to contribute funds for team operations, including hardware procurement, component shipping, and competition registration fees.

Strictly Non-Refundable: All financial contributions and payments made to the team are strictly non-refundable. Once funds are submitted, they are immediately allocated toward organizational expenses. Refunds will not be issued under any circumstances, including voluntary resignation, membership termination, or project cancellations.

3. Safety and Equipment Usage

Protocol Adherence: Strict adherence to all safety protocols is mandatory when handling UAV hardware, electronics, tools, and laboratory spaces.

Asset Ownership: All team equipment, procured components, and testing gear remain the property of the organization.

Damage Reporting: Any accidental damage to equipment or hardware must be reported immediately to team leadership. Negligent use of equipment may result in disciplinary action.

4. Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

Team Ownership: All engineering deliverables—including CAD models, CFD/FEA simulations, MATLAB/Simulink scripts, software codebases, and flight data—generated during your time on the team are the intellectual property of the organization.

Confidentiality: Members may not share proprietary team designs, technical blueprints, or competition strategies with external parties, competing teams, or unauthorized individuals without explicit permission from team leadership.

5. Chain of Command and Escalation Protocol

Hierarchical Escalation: All internal matters, technical questions, project issues, interpersonal disputes, or organizational grievances must strictly adhere to the established chain of command. Any concern must first be formally submitted to and reviewed by the Team Board. In the event an issue remains unresolved, that member retains the right to formally request a Board Meeting. If and only if the Board is unable to resolve the issue, the matter may then be formally escalated to the Team Mentor.

Strict Faculty Communication Policy: Under no circumstances is any member permitted to bypass the Team Board and/or Team Mentor to contact supervising professors or university faculty directly regarding team matters. Direct circumvention of the chain of command or unauthorized faculty outreach constitutes a zero-tolerance breach and will result in immediate and irreversible termination from the team.

6. Review and Termination

Performance: Leadership will monitor engineering benchmarks and task completion.

Termination: The team manager reserves the right to suspend or terminate membership due to consistent failure to meet project deadlines, unexcused absences, severe safety violations, breaches of escalation protocol, or any violation of these Terms and Conditions.

7. External Communications

Official Representation: Only designated leadership may officially represent the team in communications with university faculty, external delegates, technical suppliers, and competition organizers.

Sponsorships and Media: Members must not solicit sponsorships, make financial agreements, or speak to external media on behalf of the organization without explicit approval from the Team Lead.
