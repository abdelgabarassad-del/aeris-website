Join A.E.R.I.S.

Become part of our specialized aerospace sub-teams. Build custom quadcopters, model aerodynamics, code control algorithms, and shape the future of flight systems.

Recruitment Process Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Once submitted, our division heads will evaluate your profile. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a technical and behavioral interview.

Mechanical Division

Focuses on airframe design, structural optimization, rapid prototyping (3D printing, laser cutting), and balsa wood fabrication. Employs SolidWorks for CAD modeling and ANSYS for CFD analysis.

Electrical Division

Handles embedded hardware integration, custom ESC optimization, telemetry configurations, sensor interfacing, power distribution wiring, and signal diagnostics for absolute flight stability.

Software Division

Works on flight control coding, path planning, autonomous navigation algorithms, web integration, dashboard displays, telemetry databases, and real-time sensor processing models.

Non-Technical Division

Coordinates public relations, external media outreach, brand representation, corporate sponsorship outreach, logistics planning, and competition operations management.

💡 Setup Google Sheets Integration

This application form sends inputs directly to a Google Spreadsheet. To activate it:

  1. Open your **Google Spreadsheet**.
  2. Go to **Extensions** → **Apps Script**.
  3. Clear the code editor and paste the code below:
function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
  var newRow = [];
  
  newRow.push(new Date()); // Timestamp
  newRow.push(e.parameter.fullName || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.email || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.phone || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.regNumber || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.universityId || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.academicYear || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.primaryDept || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.secondaryDept || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.subInterest || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.experience || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.whyJoin || "");
  newRow.push(e.parameter.portfolioLink || "");
  
  sheet.appendRow(newRow);
  
  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({"result":"success"}))
                       .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON)
                       .setHeaders({"Access-Control-Allow-Origin": "*"});
}
  1. Click **Deploy** (top right) → **New deployment**.
  2. Choose **Web app**, set "Execute as" to **Me**, and "Who has access" to **Anyone**.
  3. Click **Deploy**, copy the **Web App URL**, and paste it in `recruitment.html` at `GOOGLE_SHEET_SCRIPT_URL`.
