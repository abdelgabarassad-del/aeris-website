Alexandria Library at AASTMT El Alamein

In an inspiring collaboration with the Alexandria Library (Bibliotheca Alexandrina), our campus hosted visiting speakers and experts for an in-depth symposium on Artificial Intelligence. The A.E.R.I.S. team had the privilege of presenting our aerospace and robotics initiatives to the attendees, while organizing interactive engineering mini-games and challenges complete with exciting prizes for the participants.
