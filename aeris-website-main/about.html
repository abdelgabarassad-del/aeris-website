About Us

A.E.R.I.S. — Advanced Engineering and Robotics in Flight Systems — is a competitive UAV team proudly based at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport (AASTMT), Alamein City campus. Founded by a passionate group of aerospace and robotics enthusiasts, the team brings together students from diverse engineering disciplines to design, build, and fly cutting-edge unmanned aerial vehicles.

Our members gain real-world experience through hands-on projects ranging from fixed-wing aircraft to multirotor drones, developing skills in aerodynamics, avionics, structural design, and autonomous flight systems. We represent AASTMT at national and international UAV competitions, consistently pushing the boundaries of student-led innovation.

Mission

Our mission is to foster the next generation of aerospace engineers by providing a collaborative, project-driven environment where students can apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges. We strive to design and manufacture high-performance UAVs, compete at the highest levels, and inspire a culture of innovation, teamwork, and engineering excellence across the AASTMT community.

Vision

We envision A.E.R.I.S. as a globally recognized student aerospace team — a launchpad for future leaders in aviation, robotics, and autonomous systems. Our goal is to continuously evolve our engineering capabilities, establish industry partnerships, and contribute meaningfully to the advancement of unmanned flight technology in the region and beyond.
